Waythread Design System

The tokens, components and rules behind Waythread, a phone-first trip planner where friends plan one route together. Everything on this page is the real system, so switch the theme above to see it change.

Less is more

Show information only when it's essential, isn't shown elsewhere, and can't be inferred at a glance.

The plan and the route lead

Stop names are the strongest text. Routes are the only blue. Controls stay quiet.

Built for sharing

Everyone on a trip can edit. People are always visible, and their changes show up as they happen.

Hand off what others do best

Navigation opens in Apple Maps or Google Maps. Waythread never rebuilds it.

Tokens come in three tiers: primitives (--wt-color-*) feed roles (--wt-surface-*, --wt-text-*, --wt-route-* …), and components use only roles and scales. A new theme redefines roles and nothing else. The full guidelines are in design-system/README.md.

Color

Color has a job or it isn't there. Blue means a route or a link. Red means "doesn't fit" or "destructive". Everything else is a warm neutral. The values below are read live from the current theme.

Primitive palette

Raw values that feed the roles. Components never use these directly.

Type

The phone's own system font, so Waythread feels native everywhere. Each token is a complete font shorthand: font: var(--wt-type-name).

Space and shape

A 4px grid. Phone side margins are --wt-space-4; the list's inner margin is --wt-space-3.

Elevation and motion

Shadows are set by role. A card gets a hairline, the sheet over the map gets a soft lift, and labels on the map float. Nothing else gets a shadow.

Motion

Sheets and toasts move with --wt-duration-base and --wt-ease-out; color changes use --wt-duration-fast. Every duration drops to 0 when reduced motion is on.

Icons

Line icons on a 24px grid with a 2px stroke and round ends, drawn in the text color. external is only for "opens another app or site". An icon-only button always has an aria-label.

WaythreadIcons.svg('car');           // the size the product uses
WaythreadIcons.svg('check', 16, 3);  // custom size and stroke

Buttons

Labels are verbs that say exactly what happens. A view has at most one primary button.

  • Primary is the one main action: Save, Add, Create, Copy.
  • Secondary is a quiet, always-available action. Add stop is the only one in the trip screen.
  • Danger always leaves a way back: an Undo toast, or a confirmation when undo isn't possible.
  • Icon buttons only for back, close and edit. Everything else gets words.

People

Each person on a trip gets one identity color, in order, for as long as they're on it. The stack beside the trip name shows who's planning and opens sharing.

  • With only one person on the trip, show the person-add icon instead of a lone avatar.

Forms

Every field has a visible label. The Place field is also the search: it shows the stop's name, and typing finds a new place.

  • Placeholders show an example, never the label.
  • Messages say what to do next: Pick a place from the search results.
  • Input text is 16px, so iPhones don't zoom in.

Patterns

How the pieces work together on the trip screen.

White Sands, NM

Fri, Nov 20

Sat, Nov 21

37 min
34 min
  1. Map first, plan on top. The map fills the screen; the list sheet opens at half height and drags to header-only or nearly full.
  2. The list steers the map. Pins for stops scrolled out of view mute (--muted), so the two read as one without numbers.
  3. Tapping links them. A pin selects its stop (.wt-stop--selected). A route highlights its drive (.wt-drive--active).
  4. Doesn't fit. A drive longer than the gap between its stops turns its time red. No badge.
  5. Hand off. Every drive opens directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps. Waythread draws routes but never gives turn-by-turn directions.
  6. Changes from others. A toast names the person and the stop, and the stop flashes once (--flash).
  7. Checking off. Checks appear only on trip days. Done stops fade and their routes turn grey.
  8. Deleting. Delete right away and offer Undo. Ask first only when undo isn't possible, like deleting a whole trip.

Accessibility

Text needs 4.5:1 against its background; large text, icons, routes and focus rings need 3:1. These ratios are measured live in the current theme.

ForegroundBackgroundRatioNeeds
  • Focus shows a 2px ring in --wt-border-focus on everything interactive.
  • Touch targets for frequent actions are at least --wt-size-touch (44px) high.
  • Color is never the only signal. A done stop also fades and fills its check; a late drive also says so to screen readers.
  • Motion drops to nothing when reduced motion is on.
