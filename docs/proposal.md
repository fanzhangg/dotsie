# Dotsie: A Minimal Trip Planner

## Motivation

I am planning a trip with my friend to White Sand, New Mexico. To share the flights and hotels I booked and the attractions we plan to go, I always need to send links or screenshots in the chat. I am lacking a centralized interface to share and track the spots in our trips.

## Concept

To plan and track our trip easily, I want to build a minimal and mobile-first web app. In the home page, an user can create a trip project like Trip to White Sand, NW, and then invite people to edit and view the project. In the project page, it should have 2 views:

- A list view to show the spots in the trip with a timestamp, like Nov 2 5:00PM, ABC hotel. All users in the project can edit or view the list. User can search a map location.

A map view to show the routes to get to the each spot, just like the Google's map's navigation UX.

## &#x20;Principles

- Less is more. Do not add non-essential design elements
- Trip routes and plan is the focus of the experience
- Co-edit a trip plan is by design not an extension
